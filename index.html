Drums

How to play

Touch to paint and play. Everything loops on the beat, then fades.

Play

  • Touch the visuals to add sound. Use more fingers.
  • Higher up is a higher note.
  • Trance synth: hold and slide.
  • Vocal chops: left, middle, right sing oh, ah, ey.

Elements

  • Blue is what you play. Amber is still sounding.
  • Auto change: a new element every 4 bars.
  • Loop all: nothing fades until you clear.
  • Hold an element to loop only that one (↻).
  • Clear fades out all you added.

Drums

  • 9 kits, from soft to hardcore.
  • Pink pills switch drums, bassline and hi-hats.
  • Auto-Break: tap for a break now. Hold to stop or restart the automatic ones.
  • Arrow next to Drums hides the drum controls.

Visuals

  • Fluid tab: 15 styles.
  • Drift changes the style by itself.
  • Arrow icon under Help: full screen.

Tips

  • No sound? Touch the screen and turn up the volume.
  • Loop a bassline, add a melody, play on top.
  • Tap Break, Clear in the build-up, come back on the drop.

This needs WebGL

The fluid runs on your graphics card, and this browser or device can’t render to floating-point textures. Try a current version of Chrome, Firefox or Safari, and check that hardware acceleration is switched on.
